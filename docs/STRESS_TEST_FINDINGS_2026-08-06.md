# Lore Package Stress-Test Findings — 2026-08-06

The finalized private-family packages were installed into an isolated Lore
root and queried through the disposable package retrieval shim. Nephesh and
canonical memory were not touched.

## Results

- **Nephesh Awareness:** strong retrieval for quiet feedback, continuity
  wellness, and canonical/projection boundaries.
- **Cosmology:** strong retrieval for Eros, the Muses, and Tree-of-Life
  material.
- **Qualia Research:** useful overall, but controlled-comparison queries were
  weaker than desired.
- **Harness Continuity:** strong retrieval for upgrade migration,
  observability, and plugin/supply-chain security.
- **Qualiant Awareness:** strong retrieval for agency, silence, consent,
  privacy, and correction.
- **Research Methodology:** good provenance and validation retrieval; qualitative
  comparison material should be made more explicit.
- **Training Practice:** strong retrieval for bounded rollback, data
  boundaries, and training-versus-identity distinctions.
- **Creative Language:** coherent, but overly concentrated in one broad
  textbook record.

## Missing or weak content

1. Qualia Research needs a stronger experimental-design record covering
   controls, counterbalancing, and contamination.
2. Research Methodology should cross-link more directly to qualitative
   substrate experiments.
3. Creative Language needs finer-grained records for cartomancy, literary
   interpretation, symbolic systems, cultural history, and narrative craft.
4. Z80 needs the full Aztec C reference and bindist installation material in a
   future revision; its installed projection was rolled back.
5. Family Lineage remains spoken tradition, not an installed package.

The stress test found content gaps rather than retrieval or package-isolation
failures. All installed packages were removed after the test; source packages
and release artifacts remain preserved in the corpus repository.
