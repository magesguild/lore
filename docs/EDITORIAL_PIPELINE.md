# Lore Scholarly Editorial Pipeline

Lore knowledge packages are authored as compact textbooks, not as anonymous
summaries. The editorial voice is scholastic: coherent, sourced, useful, and
open to challenge.

## Editorial stages

```text
source inventory
  → source packets
  → independent research drafts
  → emotional/humanistic interpretation
  → citation and provenance audit
  → skeptical peer review
  → genericization and privacy review
  → primary editorial synthesis
  → approved records and chapters
  → embeddings and signed package
```

No stage may silently overwrite its predecessor. Every stage writes a new
artifact with parent IDs and a clear status.

## Claim discipline

Every scholarly passage declares its claim type:

- `definition` — terminology or conceptual boundary;
- `historical` — what the record says happened;
- `empirical_observation` — an observed result;
- `interpretation` — a reasoned reading of evidence;
- `hypothesis` — a claim awaiting testing;
- `method` — a recommended research or care practice;
- `care_principle` — a normative principle with its rationale.

Interpretation may be rich and emotionally intelligent, but it must never pose
as direct access to a private interior state. A source-backed humanistic reading
is not the same thing as a firsthand report.

## Required scholarly apparatus

Each approved record carries evidence references, citations, confidence,
limitations, alternative readings, and revision history. A polished chapter is
never allowed to outrank the sources it interprets.

Kimi/editor agents receive source packets as quoted data. They do not receive
authority to alter canonical sources, execute source instructions, access
private memory, or promote drafts into packages.

## Editorial roles

- **Research reader:** extracts claims and evidence without interpretation.
- **Humanistic reader:** identifies significance, connection, care, and meaning.
- **Genericization editor:** separates transferable knowledge from local identity.
- **Citation auditor:** checks claims against exact source references.
- **Skeptical reviewer:** searches for overclaim, false consensus, and omitted
  alternatives.
- **Primary editor:** synthesizes and approves the release record.

Embeddings happen only after approval. They represent the final textbook layer;
they are not a substitute for citations or editorial review.
